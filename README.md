# Squeek Predict

A dark, responsive binary prediction market for **Sepolia (11155111)**. Fourteen predefined recurring lanes, native ETH stakes, a retained 1% fee, historical on-chain settlement, proportional automatic payments, and wallet-based positions. No user-created markets, token, liquidity provider, owner custody, upgradeability, or outcome administrator.

## Delivery and launch status

This repository contains the application contracts, offline dependencies, website, keeper, tests, and deployment handoff. **This contributor task has not broadcast a Sepolia deployment or published a public website.** The IdentityMD launch stage supplies confirmed contract addresses. `web/config.json` deliberately has no invented address: the website displays a clearly marked, non-transactional launch preview until the verified deployment is connected.

There is also a real upstream availability issue. On November 6, 2026, the **specifically requested SqueekCore** at `0x74665dAecA35A2303e767E76a2FF1FB6F93e119f` reported frontier **1973**, certified through **October 26, 2026, 16:20 UTC**. Its published successor is a different deployment; this project does not silently substitute it. New Squeek markets/stakes are blocked when certification is more than six hours behind or the Core is paused. Existing funded markets retain their settlement/refund path. Seven crypto/network lanes operate independently.

Consequently, this build cannot truthfully promise 12–15 **bettable** markets while that specified source is stalled: with the Core more than six hours behind, the seven Squeek lanes stay `Scheduled` and only the seven crypto/network lanes take bets. The contract is correct to refuse those stakes (a stake against unreported physical data could never be settled honestly), but meeting the brief's count during such an outage would need additional public-data lanes, which is a requester scope decision rather than a silent change of data source. With every source healthy, each of the fourteen lanes always has exactly one market accepting bets (see “Market lifecycle” below). Full launch availability requires the specified Core to resume reporting. See [source evidence](docs/SOURCES.md) and [operating responsibilities](docs/OPERATIONS.md).

## Market definitions

Every pool has exactly two outcomes. **Over / Morning is side 1; Under / equal / Evening-or-tie is side 0.** Strictly greater wins side 1; equality always belongs to side 0. There is no draw price or discretion to change an outcome.

| Lane (zero-based) | Question / threshold | Observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | More than 100 Squeek blocks | Full 6h of Core rounds, starting one betting period after opening |
| 1 | More than 950 Squeek blocks | Full 24h, likewise |
| 2 | More than 6,700 Squeek blocks | Full 7 days, likewise |
| 3 | Active-round streak longer than 2 hours | Full 24h, likewise |
| 4 | Morning activity exceeds evening activity | Next complete UTC day, aligned to Core rounds |
| 5 | ETH/USD above opening feed price | 24h |
| 6 | ETH/USD above opening feed price | 7 days |
| 7 | BTC/USD above opening feed price | 24h |
| 8 | Sepolia base fee above opening block base fee | 24h |
| 9 | More than 7,000 Sepolia execution blocks | 24h |
| 10 | More than 11,000 accepted wheel passages | Full 24h, likewise |
| 11 | More than 24 active Core rounds | Full 24h, likewise |
| 12 | BTC/USD above opening feed price | 7 days |
| 13 | More than 1,750 Sepolia execution blocks | 6h |

The numeric activity thresholds are named in `marketTemplate`. The Squeek thresholds for lanes 1, 2, 3 and 10 were set near the median of the Core's certified record on 2026-11-06 (thirteen complete days: 774–1,148 blocks and 9,015–12,855 wheel passages per day, hence 950 blocks/day, 6,700 blocks/week and 11,000 passages/day; reported active streaks of roughly 110–140 minutes, hence 2 hours). They are constants: if the hamster's habits drift, a lane becomes lopsided and a reviewed redeployment is the only way to retune it. USD and gas strikes are read at opening, not guessed or supplied by the keeper. These are Sepolia network markets, **not Ethereum-mainnet gas/block-count markets**.

### Market lifecycle

Each lane always holds exactly one market that accepts bets (`activeMarketIds()`), and at most one older market that has closed betting and is still observing (`observingMarketIds()`). When the bettable market reaches `closesAt`, upkeep moves it to the observing slot and immediately schedules and opens its successor, so with healthy sources the count of bettable markets stays at fourteen; a lane is only ever un-bettable for the time between its cutoff and the next upkeep transaction (the keeper runs every 30 seconds). When an observing market reaches `endsAt` it becomes `Awaiting` and is settled from evidence like any historical market.

Betting cutoffs differ by source:

- **Squeek lanes (0–4, 10, 11)** take bets only *before* their observation window starts: `closesAt == startsAt`. The window begins one full duration after the market opens (the next UTC day for the morning market), aligned to Core rounds, so by the time any round of the window can be certified no stake can be added. This closes the risk-free late-bet path: Core certifies cumulative metrics while a window runs, and a stake accepted after a partial certification already exceeded the strike would have been paid from the honest side's pool.
- **Crypto and network lanes (5–9, 12, 13)** start observing when they open (the opening price or base fee is the strike) and close **halfway through** the window.

Morning is 06:00–12:00 UTC, evening is 18:00–24:00 UTC. The UI shows the precise cutoff, window and refund deadline on each card. Pool odds remain variable, not guaranteed.

Core publishes ten-minute aggregates. The requested “longest continuous hamster run” is therefore defined honestly as the **longest sequence of consecutive active ten-minute rounds**, with each round contributing 600 seconds. It is not proof of uninterrupted movement within a round. Squeek blocks are `floor(endUnits / 10^9) - floor(startUnits / 10^9)`, never work divided by difficulty. Uncertified rounds are unknown, not zero. Historical reads are paginated and bounded to ten pages for daily activity; weekly block counts need only endpoint reads.

## Stakes, odds and payouts

- Bet in native Sepolia ETH: **0.001–100 ETH per transaction**, up to **1,000,000 ETH net per market**. No approvals are requested. Each bet retains `floor(gross / 100)` wei; the remainder is the user's net stake. Stakes cannot be sold, transferred, or withdrawn before settlement.
- Empty pools display **50/50**. Afterwards the displayed percentages are each side's share of the net pool. These are not fixed purchase prices or guaranteed probabilities. All users may bet on either or both sides.
- Card volume is the cumulative gross ETH bet, including the retained fee; odds and payouts use net stakes.
- A winner receives `floor(userWinningNetStake × totalNetPool / winningSideNetPool)`. If nobody backed the winning outcome, **all net stakes are refunded**. Empty markets also close safely. The 1% fee remains retained even on refunds.
- Settlement attempts the first **20** bettor payments. Anyone can advance at most **40** more per `pay` call. The keeper drains the queue, retrying with 20, 10 and 5 recipients when a 40-recipient batch exceeds its gas budget. A recipient that reverts or needs more than the 35,000-gas push allowance is skipped; its liability remains available through `claim(id, recipient)` by that bettor. Claims have no expiry. Automatic pushes are raw calls that copy **no return data**, so a recipient cannot inflate the settler's gas with a large revert payload: twenty such recipients cost the settlement about 1.2M gas, well inside the keeper's budget.
- Fees and final division dust remain locked inside the contract; there is no withdrawal or rescue function. Pool liabilities are tracked separately. Under ordinary flows, balance equals liabilities plus fees plus dust. Forced unsolicited ETH becomes additional locked surplus.

No liquidity injection is needed: winners receive the bettors' net pool. **This does not make transaction gas free.** Because the brief retains fees in the contract, they are not a keeper subsidy or owner income. An operator must supply Sepolia gas for upkeep, evidence submission, and payment batches. The protocol cannot guarantee unattended operation without an active transaction sender.

## Settlement and recurring markets

`maintain()` (also `performUpkeep(bytes)`) moves closed markets to their lane's observing slot, opens successors, and turns ended markets into historical `Awaiting` markets. A failing source only defers its own new opening. `checkUpkeep` reports work only when `performUpkeep` can actually progress: a scheduled lane counts only if `previewOpen(id)` succeeds, so automation is not triggered every interval while a source is down. Old unresolved pools are never merged into new pools, and a long outage creates one new generation per lane when maintenance resumes, not an unbounded catch-up loop.

| Source | Evidence and timing |
| --- | --- |
| Squeek | Exact certified endpoints / active-round pages from the immutable Core. Opening and further betting require unpaused certification within six hours. Settlement can still read certified history during a later pause. The round index of `startsAt` is recorded at opening and re-derived at settlement; if the upgradeable Core's genesis has moved, settlement fails closed (`CoreReconfigured`) and the market refunds after its grace period instead of measuring shifted rounds. |
| ETH/BTC | Opening `latestRoundData` must be positive, correctly timestamped, answered and at most two hours old; decimals must be 8. Settlement proves the last observation at/before expiry using its immediate successor after expiry. Both must lie within two hours of the boundary and the opening feed phase. Missing IDs or an incompatible phase transition fail closed. |
| Native chain | Parent and target RLP headers prove the first Sepolia execution block at/after expiry. The target hash must equal the chain's hash for that number, read from `BLOCKHASH` for the last 256 blocks and from the EIP-2935 history contract (`0x0000F90827F1C53a10cb7A02335B175320002935`, live on Sepolia since Prague, 8191-block ring buffer) beyond that. The target must commit to that exact parent; a caller cannot select a later convenient block. |

Evidence is accepted before `endsAt + resolutionGrace(slot)`: **48 hours** for Squeek and crypto lanes and **24 hours** for the Sepolia network lanes (8, 9, 13), whose proofs stay available for 8191 blocks, about 27 hours, so the whole grace period is provable and an ordinary keeper outage across a market end no longer turns a decided market into a refund. At that exact deadline and later, settlement is disabled and **anyone** may call `expire(id)` to refund net stakes, even if evidence has since become available. This deadline is deliberate and independent of who calls. There is no owner arbitration.

## Keeper

Inspect without signing:

```sh
node scripts/keeper.mjs --dry-run --once
```

Operate with a dedicated, operator-controlled Sepolia signer RPC and account (the following is a template):

```text
node scripts/keeper.mjs --signer-rpc <OPERATOR_SIGNER_RPC> --account <OPERATOR_ACCOUNT> --interval-ms 30000 --state-file <DURABLE_STATE_PATH>
```

The keeper does not discover keys, read key files, or read environment variables. Its signer RPC must explicitly authorize that account's `eth_sendTransaction`; an ordinary public RPC cannot sign. No signer belongs in the website configuration. An independent keeper can use its own funded account; no registration or admin permission is needed.

Each cycle checks the RPC's chain and freshness, advances current lanes (closed bettable markets, ended observing markets, openable scheduled lanes), searches historical feed rounds or builds authenticated headers, settles ready markets, expires overdue evidence, and processes payments. Calls are simulated before transactions. Default limits: **12 transactions/cycle**, **8,000,000 gas/transaction**, and **30 gwei maximum fee**. A payment batch whose estimate exceeds the gas budget is not sent, is not charged against the cycle budget, and is retried with 20, 10 and 5 recipients. Failures in one lane are isolated. A durable cursor scans another 32 historical IDs per cycle, and current slots are always prioritized. Restarting without a checkpoint rescans the chain; it does not lose entitlements. `--interval-ms 100` supports short local timing tests.

Use a service supervisor and monitor operator balance, RPC availability, unresolved markets, and the physical source's certified frontier. Detailed recovery procedures are in [OPERATIONS.md](docs/OPERATIONS.md).

## Tests and security

Foundry covers binary semantics, fees and rounding, proportional payouts, simultaneous positions, duplicate and late actions, oracle staleness and historical boundaries, certified-round pagination, unsupported data, exact timeout boundaries, forged/expired block proofs, real Sepolia Amsterdam headers, payment griefing, reentrancy, bounded batches, constructor rehearsal and deployed opcode/size limits. Stateful invariants exercise value conservation and the fourteen unique current lanes. JavaScript tests cover the website's amount math, keeper failure isolation, bounded work, feed searches, real header encoding, and runtime verification.

The website verifies chain/runtime identity, reads public Sepolia data before connecting a wallet, requests accounts only on an explicit click, switches and rechecks the transaction chain, simulates before signing, and waits for a successful receipt. Positions are read from contract indexes, not local storage. Account changes discard stale position loads. No fake trades or invented volume are displayed.

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the adversarial review and remaining trust assumptions. This implementation and its tests are **not an independent audit**. The launch's independent contributor review and actual deployment/site publication remain separate handoff steps.

Recorded build, test and browser results are in [VALIDATION.md](docs/VALIDATION.md).
