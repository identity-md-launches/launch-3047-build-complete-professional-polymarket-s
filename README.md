# Squeek Predict

A dark, responsive binary prediction market for **Sepolia (11155111)**. Fourteen predefined recurring lanes, native ETH stakes, a retained 1% fee, historical on-chain settlement, proportional automatic payments, and wallet-based positions. No user-created markets, token, liquidity provider, owner custody, upgradeability, or outcome administrator.

## Delivery and launch status

This repository contains the application contracts, offline dependencies, website, keeper, tests, and deployment handoff. **This contributor task has not broadcast a Sepolia deployment or published a public website.** The IdentityMD launch stage supplies confirmed contract addresses. `web/config.json` deliberately has no invented address: the website displays a clearly marked, non-transactional launch preview until the verified deployment is connected.

There is also a real upstream availability issue. On November 6, 2026, the **specifically requested SqueekCore** at `0x74665dAecA35A2303e767E76a2FF1FB6F93e119f` reported frontier **1973**, certified through **October 26, 2026, 16:20 UTC**. Its published successor is a different deployment; this project does not silently substitute it. New Squeek markets/stakes are blocked when certification is more than six hours behind or the Core is paused. Existing funded markets retain their settlement/refund path. Seven crypto/network lanes operate independently.

Consequently, this build cannot truthfully promise 12–15 **bettable** markets while that specified source is stalled. It preserves **exactly fourteen current slots**, including waiting and observing slots, and renews them on upkeep. Full launch availability requires the specified Core to resume reporting. See [source evidence](docs/SOURCES.md) and [operating responsibilities](docs/OPERATIONS.md).

## Market definitions

Every pool has exactly two outcomes. **Over / Morning is side 1; Under / equal / Evening-or-tie is side 0.** Strictly greater wins side 1; equality always belongs to side 0. There is no draw price or discretion to change an outcome.

| Lane (zero-based) | Question / threshold | Observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | More than 100 Squeek blocks | Next full 6h of Core rounds |
| 1 | More than 400 Squeek blocks | Next full 24h |
| 2 | More than 2,800 Squeek blocks | Next full 7 days |
| 3 | Active-round streak longer than 30 minutes | Next full 24h |
| 4 | Morning activity exceeds evening activity | Next complete UTC day, aligned to Core rounds |
| 5 | ETH/USD above opening feed price | 24h |
| 6 | ETH/USD above opening feed price | 7 days |
| 7 | BTC/USD above opening feed price | 24h |
| 8 | Sepolia base fee above opening block base fee | 24h |
| 9 | More than 7,000 Sepolia execution blocks | 24h |
| 10 | More than 5,000 accepted wheel passages | Next full 24h |
| 11 | More than 24 active Core rounds | Next full 24h |
| 12 | BTC/USD above opening feed price | 7 days |
| 13 | More than 1,750 Sepolia execution blocks | 6h |

The numeric activity thresholds are named in `marketTemplate`. USD and gas strikes are read at opening, not guessed or supplied by the keeper. These are Sepolia network markets, **not Ethereum-mainnet gas/block-count markets**.

Except for the morning market, bets close **halfway through the observation window**. Morning bets close at the start of its day; morning is 06:00–12:00 UTC, evening is 18:00–24:00 UTC. The UI shows the precise opening, cutoff, expiry, and refund deadline on each card. Closing before expiry prevents bets on the final published result, though cumulative activity may cross its threshold earlier; pool odds remain variable, not guaranteed.

Core publishes ten-minute aggregates. The requested “longest continuous hamster run” is therefore defined honestly as the **longest sequence of consecutive active ten-minute rounds**, with each round contributing 600 seconds. It is not proof of uninterrupted movement within a round. Squeek blocks are `floor(endUnits / 10^9) - floor(startUnits / 10^9)`, never work divided by difficulty. Uncertified rounds are unknown, not zero. Historical reads are paginated and bounded to ten pages for daily activity; weekly block counts need only endpoint reads.

## Stakes, odds and payouts

- Bet in native Sepolia ETH: **0.001–100 ETH per transaction**, up to **1,000,000 ETH net per market**. No approvals are requested. Each bet retains `floor(gross / 100)` wei; the remainder is the user's net stake. Stakes cannot be sold, transferred, or withdrawn before settlement.
- Empty pools display **50/50**. Afterwards the displayed percentages are each side's share of the net pool. These are not fixed purchase prices or guaranteed probabilities. All users may bet on either or both sides.
- Card volume is the cumulative gross ETH bet, including the retained fee; odds and payouts use net stakes.
- A winner receives `floor(userWinningNetStake × totalNetPool / winningSideNetPool)`. If nobody backed the winning outcome, **all net stakes are refunded**. Empty markets also close safely. The 1% fee remains retained even on refunds.
- Settlement attempts the first **20** bettor payments. Anyone can advance at most **40** more per `pay` call. The keeper drains the queue. A recipient that reverts or needs more than the 35,000-gas push allowance is skipped; its liability remains available through `claim(id, recipient)` by that bettor. Claims have no expiry.
- Fees and final division dust remain locked inside the contract; there is no withdrawal or rescue function. Pool liabilities are tracked separately. Under ordinary flows, balance equals liabilities plus fees plus dust. Forced unsolicited ETH becomes additional locked surplus.

No liquidity injection is needed: winners receive the bettors' net pool. **This does not make transaction gas free.** Because the brief retains fees in the contract, they are not a keeper subsidy or owner income. An operator must supply Sepolia gas for upkeep, evidence submission, and payment batches. The protocol cannot guarantee unattended operation without an active transaction sender.

## Settlement and recurring markets

`maintain()` (also `performUpkeep(bytes)`) rolls expired current slots into historical `Awaiting` markets and creates new scheduled markets of the same template. A failing source only defers its own new opening. Old unresolved pools are never merged into new pools, and a long outage creates one new generation per lane when maintenance resumes, not an unbounded catch-up loop.

| Source | Evidence and timing |
| --- | --- |
| Squeek | Exact certified endpoints / active-round pages from the immutable Core. Opening and further betting require unpaused certification within six hours. Settlement can still read certified history during a later pause. |
| ETH/BTC | Opening `latestRoundData` must be positive, correctly timestamped, answered and at most two hours old; decimals must be 8. Settlement proves the last observation at/before expiry using its immediate successor after expiry. Both must lie within two hours of the boundary and the opening feed phase. Missing IDs or an incompatible phase transition fail closed. |
| Native chain | Parent and target RLP headers prove the first Sepolia execution block at/after expiry. The target hash must equal `blockhash(number)` and commit to that exact parent. A caller cannot select a later convenient block. Submit within 256 execution blocks, generally about 51 minutes. The keeper runs every 30 seconds and leaves a one-block submission margin. |

Evidence is accepted before `endsAt + 48h`. At that exact deadline and later, settlement is disabled and **anyone** may call `expire(id)` to refund net stakes, even if evidence has since become available. This deadline is deliberate and independent of who calls. Native proof windows can expire sooner; funds still become refundable at the same 48h deadline. There is no owner arbitration or recovery of consumed BLOCKHASH history.

## Keeper

Inspect without signing:

```sh
node scripts/keeper.mjs --dry-run --once
```

Operate with a dedicated, operator-controlled Sepolia signer RPC and account (the following is a template):

```text
node scripts/keeper.mjs --signer-rpc <OPERATOR_SIGNER_RPC> --account <OPERATOR_ACCOUNT> --interval-ms 30000 --state-file <DURABLE_STATE_PATH>
```

The keeper does not discover keys, read key files, or read environment variables. Its signer RPC must explicitly authorize that account's `eth_sendTransaction`; an ordinary public RPC cannot sign. No signer belongs in the website configuration. An independent keeper can use its own funded account; no registration or admin permission is needed.

Each cycle checks the RPC's chain and freshness, advances current lanes, searches historical feed rounds or builds authenticated headers, settles ready markets, expires overdue evidence, and processes payments. Calls are simulated before transactions. Default limits: **12 transactions/cycle**, **8,000,000 gas/transaction**, and **30 gwei maximum fee**. Failures in one lane are isolated. A durable cursor scans another 32 historical IDs per cycle, and current slots are always prioritized. Restarting without a checkpoint rescans the chain; it does not lose entitlements. `--interval-ms 100` supports short local timing tests.

Use a service supervisor and monitor operator balance, RPC availability, unresolved markets, and the physical source's certified frontier. Detailed recovery procedures are in [OPERATIONS.md](docs/OPERATIONS.md).

## Tests and security

Foundry covers binary semantics, fees and rounding, proportional payouts, simultaneous positions, duplicate and late actions, oracle staleness and historical boundaries, certified-round pagination, unsupported data, exact timeout boundaries, forged/expired block proofs, real Sepolia Amsterdam headers, payment griefing, reentrancy, bounded batches, constructor rehearsal and deployed opcode/size limits. Stateful invariants exercise value conservation and the fourteen unique current lanes. JavaScript tests cover the website's amount math, keeper failure isolation, bounded work, feed searches, real header encoding, and runtime verification.

The website verifies chain/runtime identity, reads public Sepolia data before connecting a wallet, requests accounts only on an explicit click, switches and rechecks the transaction chain, simulates before signing, and waits for a successful receipt. Positions are read from contract indexes, not local storage. Account changes discard stale position loads. No fake trades or invented volume are displayed.

See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the adversarial review and remaining trust assumptions. This implementation and its tests are **not an independent audit**. The launch's independent contributor review and actual deployment/site publication remain separate handoff steps.

Recorded build, test and browser results are in [VALIDATION.md](docs/VALIDATION.md).
