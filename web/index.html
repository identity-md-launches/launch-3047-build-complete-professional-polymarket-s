SIMPLE RULES. VISIBLE OUTCOMES.

A view. A pool. An outcome.

Squeek Predict is a binary parimutuel market on Sepolia. All stakes and payouts use testnet ETH.

01

Choose your signal

Fourteen predefined lanes cover Squeek activity, ETH and BTC prices, and Sepolia network metrics. Anyone can bet; nobody can add their own markets.

02

Back a side

Each new pool starts at 50/50. Your bet pays a 1% fee, retained in the contract. The remaining 99% joins your chosen side. There are no fixed-price shares or early exits.

03

Follow the result

The contract verifies its data, then splits the net pool among winning stakes. The keeper submits evidence, advances payments and opens the next market in the same lane.

How much can I win?

Your payout is your net winning stake divided by all net winning stakes, multiplied by the full net pool. The displayed estimate changes as more bets arrive.

Your winning stake ÷ winning side × net pool

Example: 1 ETH versus 3 ETH creates a 3.96 ETH net pool. If the 1 ETH side wins and has one bettor, that bettor receives 3.96 ETH. Rounding is down to wei.

What if something goes wrong?

If no stake backed the winning side, everyone gets their net stake back. If evidence is missing after the market’s grace period (48 hours; 24 hours for Sepolia network markets), anyone can trigger the same net refund. Failed payments stay claimable to a recipient you choose. Fees stay in the contract.

What does “automatic” mean?

A keeper runs continuously and submits transactions. The contracts cannot wake themselves up. Anyone can perform the same work, and an unavailable data source cannot stop other market lanes.

There are always fourteen lanes, each with one market taking bets. When betting closes, that market keeps observing and its successor opens immediately, so a lane is never without a bettable market while its source is healthy. Observing and expired markets are listed with your positions.

Where do outcomes come from?

Squeek uses certified ten-minute rounds from the specified SqueekCore. Crypto uses Chainlink’s historical USD feed rounds. Network markets use authenticated Sepolia block headers.

“Longest run” means consecutive active ten-minute rounds. A network’s gas fee means its block base fee. Full UTC windows and rules are available on every card.

Keep the markets moving

Run permissionless upkeep to open available markets and renew expired slots. Settlement and payments are handled by the keeper, with claim and refund recovery on My Positions.
